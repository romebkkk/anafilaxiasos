## 🌟 Why this matters
Anaphylaxis is an unpredictable, rapidly progressing life-threatening allergic reaction. **Intramuscular adrenaline (epinephrine) is the only first-line treatment**, yet in real emergencies patients, teachers, and caregivers freeze or hesitate to administer it due to panic and lack of training. Every minute of delay drastically increases mortality.

**AnafilaxiaSOS** offers an immediate symptom detector based on World Allergy Organization (WAO 2020) criteria, a zero-anxiety interactive auto-injector simulator ("Blue to the sky, orange to the thigh"), and a printable emergency passport.

---

## ⚡ Key Features
* **WAO 2020 Anaphylaxis Diagnostic Criteria:** Differentiates mild local hives from multisystem airway/cardiovascular emergencies.
* **Interactive Auto-Injector Trainer:** Step-by-step muscle memory practice with countdown timer for EpiPen / Jext / Altellus devices.
* **Printable Emergency Allergy Passport:** Wallet card with patient info, allergen list, and emergency contacts.

## 🇪🇸 Español

### ¿Por qué importa?
La anafilaxia puede causar asfixia o shock en cuestión de minutos. La adrenalina intramuscular en el muslo es el único tratamiento que salva vidas, pero el miedo y la falta de práctica hacen que muchas personas duden en usar el autoinyector.

**AnafilaxiaSOS** ayuda a detectar la anafilaxia al instante y permite practicar con el simulador interactivo para saber exactamente cómo actuar ante una emergencia real.
